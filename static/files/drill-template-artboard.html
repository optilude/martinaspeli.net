    Enclosure artboard sizes (Side A/B/C/D/E, in mm) are taken from Tayda's published UV-printing template table, since those are exactly the "canvas" dimensions Tayda expects for a print/design file on each side — this is why they're intentionally a little smaller than the enclosure's true outer size (rounded corners excluded). For an enclosure not listed, choose Custom and enter the artboard size yourself. Coordinates follow Tayda's drill convention: (0,0) is the center of the side, +X is right, +Y is up. Holes are drawn as unfilled circles at true diameter; shape rows are drawn as cut lines. This tool runs entirely in your browser — nothing you upload is sent anywhere.
